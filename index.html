Hot Spots on the Hill

Alcohol and drug arrests in Chapel Hill, 2015 vs. 2024

Alcohol and drug arrests fell by about 82%. Chapel Hill police made 519 alcohol and drug arrests in 2015 and 91 in 2024. Alcohol arrests dropped from 359 to 30, and drug arrests from 160 to 61. In 2015, arrests formed a large, dense cluster downtown and spread into many spots to the north and east. In 2024, downtown has a much smaller cluster and the rest are scattered, smaller spots.

Not just fewer arrests overall. Total arrests also fell, from 1,818 in 2015 to 991 in 2024 (about 45%), but alcohol and drug arrests fell faster. Together they made up about 29% of all arrests in 2015 and about 9% in 2024. Alcohol arrests went from about 20% of all arrests to 3%, while drug arrests went from about 9% to 6%.

How to read the maps. Both maps use the same area and the same colors. Colored areas are within about a city block (120 m) of at least one arrest, and darker means more arrests close together. Each arrest colors the area around it, and the colors add up where arrests are close together. The legend numbers are a measure of that buildup, not a count of arrests: one arrest alone shows up as about 61, and 400 means roughly 7 arrests piled up in the same small area.

Source: Chapel Hill Police arrest records. Arrests made by UNC Police and arrests without a recorded date are not included. Percentages of all arrests use arrests with a recorded date.
